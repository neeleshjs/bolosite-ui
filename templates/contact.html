Questions, answered

A few things you may be wondering.

Still have a question? bolosite.1@gmail.com

Create a owner account, add your website details and approved content, then paste one script tag using your unique site ID and token. The workspace controls business knowledge, allowed actions, and usage.

After the free 3-day trial, billing is ₹200 per month plus ₹1 for each billable service request. Your portal shows every charge and warns you before the ₹1,000 monthly safety limit.

You have a 7-day payment window. After that, service pauses but your configuration and knowledge are not deleted. Payment reactivates BoloSite.

BoloSite uses approved pages, products, pricing, FAQs, documents, and sales notes that belong to your website. You can update this information from the owner portal.

BoloSite currently supports Hindi and English with automatic detection. Visitors can ask by speech or typing, so the experience still works when speaking is not possible.
