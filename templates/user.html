01
The normal website problem

You visit a website to find a solution. Then the website makes you do the work.

You click through menus, scan long sections, open multiple pages, compare details, and still wonder where the right answer is. The solution may already exist on the website, but it is hidden behind messy navigation, scattered content, and unclear buttons.

The feeling visitors know You get distracted, your time is wasted, your patience drops, and you may leave before finding the right product, answer, or knowledge.

BoloSite gives you a direct way to ask the website instead of hunting through it alone.

02
What BoloSite changes

Instead of searching the website, you talk to it.

Find the right answer faster

Ask what you need and let BoloSite guide you to the relevant information, page, product, policy, or form.

Understand the page

When content feels complicated, ask for a simple explanation in natural language.

Move without confusion

Say or type things like "show pricing", "open contact", or "which product is right for me?"

Type when privacy matters

If you cannot speak because you are in public, at work, in a noisy place, or asking something private, typing works too.

Use your natural language

Ask in Hindi, English, or the mix you normally use. The goal is comfort, not perfect commands.

Reach the next step

BoloSite can help you continue from the answer to the correct section, product, form, or action approved by the website owner.

Quick start

From arriving to getting help.

These seven steps work on any website where the BoloSite orb appears.

Open the BoloSite-powered website

Look for the round BoloSite orb near the bottom-right corner.

Find the orb

Allow microphone access if you want voice

Your browser may ask for permission after you interact with the orb. You can choose text instead if speaking is not suitable.

Your choice

Say “Hello” or “Hi” — or tap once

This wakes BoloSite and starts voice listening. Wait for the orb to turn green before giving a longer request.

Voice start

Double-click or double-tap for text chat

The chat panel opens with a text box, quick suggestions, voice controls, history, and answer tools.

Text start

Ask in your own words

Try “Show me pricing,” “Explain this simply,” or “Which option fits a small team?”

No fixed syntax

Watch, listen, and follow the result

BoloSite may answer, scroll, open an approved section, or guide you into an approved form.

Answer + action

Continue, interrupt, close, or let it rest

Ask a follow-up, speak over a reply to redirect it, close the panel with ×, or leave it inactive so it sleeps.

You stay in control

What happens after you ask

One conversation, five clear stages.

The website does the searching work while you stay focused on your need.

01 · LISTEN

Your request is captured

BoloSite receives your spoken words or typed message and detects the language being used.

02 · UNDERSTAND

Your intent is identified

It works out whether you need information, navigation, comparison, support, or a form action.

03 · FIND

Approved knowledge is searched

It checks the website content and business knowledge provided by the owner.

04 · ANSWER

A clear reply is prepared

The answer is matched to your language and the context of your conversation.

05 · ACT

The website may move with you

If approved, BoloSite can open, scroll, click, guide, or begin filling a form.

OWNER CONTROL

Not every site enables every action

If an action is unavailable, ask for the nearest alternative or use the website controls directly.

Writing mode

Let BoloSite help with an approved form.

You provide the information, BoloSite fills the mapped fields, and you remain responsible for reviewing and confirming submission.

1

Ask for the form

Say or type, “Help me fill the contact form.”

2

Answer naturally

Give one field or several fields together: “My name is Asha and my email is…”

3

Correct anything

Say, “Change my phone number to…” or “Use my work email instead.”

4

Review the visible form

Check names, contact details, selections, and the message before sending.

5

Confirm or cancel

Submit only when you are ready, or say that you want to cancel the form process.

Orb colour guide

The colour tells you what BoloSite is doing.

If you are unsure, check the orb and the status word inside the chat header.

Grey

Sleeping

Say “Hello/Hi” or tap once.

Purple

Ready or idle, when shown

Start speaking or open chat.

Green

Listening

Speak naturally now.

Cyan

Thinking

Wait for the answer.

Yellow

Speaking

Listen or interrupt politely.
Close, sleep, and wake again

Stopping the panel and resting the assistant are different.

01 - CLOSE CHAT

Tap the X button

This hides the text panel and its history view. It does not immediately put the voice assistant to sleep.

02 - AUTO SLEEP

Leave it inactive

When chat is closed and BoloSite is not thinking or speaking, it sleeps after about 60 seconds of inactivity.

03 - WAKE AGAIN

Greet or tap

Say “Hello” or “Hi,” or tap the orb. If voice input is off, open chat and turn the mic back on or keep typing.

Troubleshooting

If something does not work, try this.

Most issues can be solved by checking permission, colour, connection, or whether the website owner enabled the action.

The microphone permission was blocked

Continue with text chat, or open your browser’s site permissions and allow the microphone for this website. Reload the page after changing permission.

BoloSite does not react to my voice

Check that the mic icon is on, the orb is green, and no other app is using the microphone. Move closer, reduce background noise, or switch to text.

The orb is cyan for a while

Cyan means BoloSite is thinking. Wait for the current request to finish. If the connection fails, use the text panel to retry once with a shorter request.

It cannot open a page or perform an action

The action may not be approved on this website. Ask for the information instead, request the nearest available section, or use the website’s visible navigation.

I am in a noisy or private place

Double-tap the orb to open chat, switch voice input off with the mic button, and type. Voice output can also be stopped from the answer’s speaker control.

A form field looks wrong

Tell BoloSite exactly what to change, then inspect the visible form yourself. Do not confirm submission until every important field is correct.

What should I avoid sharing?

Share only information required for your task. Treat passwords, payment data, OTPs, health information, and private identifiers carefully, and verify the website before entering them.
